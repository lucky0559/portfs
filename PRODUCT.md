# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, engineering leads, founders, and prospective clients who need to quickly understand Lucky Angelo's capabilities, experience, and the kinds of products he can build.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio turns a broad software-development background into a clear, credible story. It should help a visitor scan the developer's focus, see evidence across real projects, understand the depth behind the stack, and start a conversation with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Clear, thoughtful, quietly technical. The experience should feel capable and personal, with enough visual character to be memorable without making the work harder to understand.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic developer-template patterns: noisy neon-on-black surfaces, oversized motion for its own sake, skewed or hard-to-use navigation, repeated identical cards, vague AI copy, and dense tool lists that read like a keyword dump.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the kind of problems Lucky solves, not a list of technologies.
2. Make depth scannable: give experience, tools, and project context distinct visual roles.
3. Use specificity and restraint to build trust.
4. Keep the path from first impression to contact short and obvious.
5. Let motion add orientation and feedback, never friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals: semantic landmarks, keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, descriptive labels, touch-friendly controls, and a reduced-motion path for animated content. Do not communicate meaning through color alone.
